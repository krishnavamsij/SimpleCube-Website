What Is Customer 360?

Customer 360 is Salesforce's comprehensive approach to unifying customer data across every touchpoint — creating a single, cohesive view of each customer so organizations can personalize interactions, anticipate needs, and build lasting relationships. The latest enhancements bring four powerful capabilities to the platform:

Unified Customer Profiles
AI-driven insights aggregate data from multiple sources into a complete picture of customer interactions, preferences, and behaviors.
Enhanced Data Integration
Seamless integration with third-party applications ensures that sales, service, and marketing data is always readily accessible in one place.
Personalization at Scale
Tools that tailor marketing messages and service offerings to individual customer data — enhancing relevance and engagement at any volume.
Real-Time Analytics
Powerful analytics that monitor customer interactions as they happen, enabling proactive engagement strategies before opportunities are missed.

Hyniva's Vision: Putting Customer 360 to Work for Clients

Hyniva's approach to Customer 360 goes beyond implementation. We align every feature to specific client business goals — developing roadmaps, optimizing processes, and deploying proprietary accelerators that reduce time-to-market and cost. Here is how we plan to leverage these innovations, illustrated through the lens of the banking sector:

1
Tailored Customer Experiences
Using unified customer profiles, Hyniva creates personalized marketing strategies that speak to each individual customer's history, preferences, and current context.
Banking example: Crafting unique mortgage rate offers based on a customer's financial history and current market conditions — driving higher engagement and satisfaction.
2
Data-Driven Decision Making
Real-time analytics empower clients to make informed decisions based on current customer behaviors and trends — not yesterday's data.
Banking example: Analyzing transaction data to uncover spending patterns and adjust credit card rewards programs to better align with what customers actually value.
3
Streamlined Communication
Enhanced data integration creates seamless communication across departments — ensuring every team works from the same customer profile and speaks with one consistent voice.
Banking example: Marketing, sales, and service teams accessing shared profiles to deliver consistent, coordinated responses to loan product inquiries.
4
Proactive Customer Engagement
AI-powered insights help clients anticipate customer needs and address concerns before they escalate — shifting the relationship from reactive to genuinely proactive.
Banking example: Using predictive analytics to identify customers at risk of overdrawing, then proactively offering overdraft protection options before an issue occurs.
5
Improved ROI on Marketing Campaigns
More precise targeting and personalization translate directly into higher campaign returns — because every message reaches the right customer at the right moment.
Banking example: A targeted email campaign promoting new investment services to customers already showing interest in wealth management — significantly increasing conversion rates.
6
Scalable Solutions
As businesses grow, their customer data needs grow with them. Hyniva leverages Customer 360's native scalability to ensure the platform expands seamlessly alongside each client's operations.
Banking example: Integrating new branches or digital services into existing customer profiles as a bank scales — maintaining a cohesive experience without disruption.
