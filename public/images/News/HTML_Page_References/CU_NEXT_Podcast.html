CU NEXT Podcast: Voices Driving
Credit Union Innovation

Hyniva is excited to introduce CU NEXT Podcast. A new leadership podcast series created for the Credit Union community.

Over the past few years, our team has been in conversations with leaders across the industry and one theme continues to emerge consistently: Digital Transformation in Credit Unions is becoming increasingly complex.

Most transformation initiatives do not fail because of technology alone. They struggle because of:

  • Too many disconnected vendors
  • Fragmented systems and platforms
  • Lack of a unified digital strategy
  • Challenges balancing innovation with trust and compliance

At the same time, the industry itself is evolving rapidly.

Credit Unions are navigating changing member expectations, increasing competition from FinTechs and the growing need to attract younger generations such as Gen Z and Gen Alpha through modern digital experiences.

This shift is raising important industry-wide questions:

  • How can Credit Unions grow while competing on digital experience?
  • How can organizations prepare for the next generation of members?
  • How can AI be adopted responsibly and securely?
  • How can the industry move toward cognitive banking without losing trust?

These are not isolated concerns. They are conversations happening across the entire financial services ecosystem.

Why We Started CU NEXT Podcast

To help address these evolving challenges, Hyniva launched CU NEXT Podcast, a platform designed to bring together real voices, real experiences and practical insights from industry leaders.

CU NEXT Podcast is a leadership-focused series where CXOs and senior executives share their perspectives on:

  • Digital transformation in Credit Unions
  • Building trusted and scalable platforms
  • AI adoption in financial services
  • Member experience innovation
  • Cognitive banking and intelligent automation
  • Compliance, governance and trust in modern banking

Unlike traditional presentations or industry panels, CU NEXT Podcast focuses on authentic conversations and practical experiences from leaders who are actively driving transformation within their organizations.

Real Conversations. Real Experiences.

The goal of CU NEXT Podcast is simple:

to create meaningful industry conversations that go beyond theory.

No slides. No scripted presentations. Just honest discussions about the opportunities, challenges and future of Credit Union innovation.

As the financial services industry continues to evolve, shared learning will play a critical role in helping organizations navigate digital transformation successfully.

Looking Ahead

At Hyniva, we believe the future of banking will be shaped by organizations that can combine innovation, intelligence, trust and member-centric experiences.

Through CU NEXT Podcast, we aim to create a space where industry leaders can share insights, inspire new thinking and help shape the future of Credit Unions together.
